Short questions for the start of class. Students write their answer by hand, take a picture and upload it, and get feedback right away. There are four types: Definition, Explain It in Your Own Words, Derivation/Computation, and Provide the Formula. The topics are Statistics, Linear Algebra and Differential Equations.

Each question is placed on three axes, each scored from 0 to 5:

- **Transcription:** how hard it is for an AI to turn a photo of the handwritten answer into text.
- **Review:** how hard it is for an AI to judge the transcribed answer and give feedback.
- **Execution:** how hard it is for the student to produce a correct answer in a few minutes.
